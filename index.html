Who I Am

Engineering at the Intersection
of Software & Systems

I'm Abdaljalil Debow — widely known as Fadi Debow — a Senior Full Stack Developer, IT Manager, and Signal Systems Engineer based in Latakia, Syria, with over a decade of experience building production-grade systems that scale.

My expertise spans the full stack: from architecting backend microservices with Node.js, NestJS, gRPC, and Laravel, to crafting fluid frontends with Vue.js, React.js, and Next.js, to shipping cross-platform mobile apps with Flutter.

Beyond software, I engineer real-time SDR-based signal intelligence systems in C++ and Python — a rare intersection of software craftsmanship and hardware integration. I currently serve as Head of IT at a government directorate, driving complete digital transformation across the institution.

I'm also the founder of Code With Fadi — an educational programming channel empowering the next generation of developers in Arabic.
